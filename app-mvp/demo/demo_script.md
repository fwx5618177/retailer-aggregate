# Demo Script - SEA Retailer Top Selling Intelligence

## Story 1: TikTok Brand Explosion - Opportunity Discovery

**Scenario:** A personal care brand is exploding on TikTok but we haven't covered it yet.

### Steps:
1. **Overview Page** - Note the overall match rate (~60%) showing gaps in coverage
2. **Alerts Page** - Find a "rank_jump" alert: "Senka Perfect Whip jumped from #150 to #12 on TikTok"
3. **Top Items Page** - Filter by brand "Senka", platform "TikTok" - see high rank, strong reviews
4. **Top Items Page** - Switch to platform "Shopee" - note Senka has fewer items or lower rankings
5. **Review Queue** - See the Senka items flagged as "needs_review" due to moderate confidence
6. **Action**: Mark as "harvestable" - this is a sourcing opportunity

**Key Insight:** TikTok is driving demand for Senka products. Our offline stores should stock them.

---

## Story 2: Cross-Platform Price Anomaly

**Scenario:** Same product has significantly different pricing across platforms.

### Steps:
1. **Overview Page** - Note price band distribution differences between TikTok and Shopee
2. **Alerts Page** - Find a "price_anomaly" alert: "Dove Body Wash 500ml priced 30% lower on TikTok"
3. **Top Items Page** - Filter by brand "Dove", see the matched item pair
4. **Item Detail** - Click through to see price history and the matched Shopee item
5. **Review Queue** - This pair is "auto_accepted" (high confidence match)
6. **Action**: Flag for pricing team review - competitive pressure from TikTok pricing

**Key Insight:** Platform pricing pressure may affect our offline pricing strategy.

---

## Demo Flow (5 minutes):
1. Start on Overview (30s) - show KPIs and overlap
2. Navigate to Top Items (60s) - browse, filter, show detail
3. Navigate to Alerts (60s) - show 2-3 alerts with actions
4. Navigate to Review (90s) - review a pair, submit decision
5. Wrap up (30s) - discuss scale strategy and next steps
